eJahan Moderators Manifesto

Version 1.2 (Last update: 2013/03/29)

0. These laws are eJahan moderators' manifesto, determined by eJahan's top-level administration team (HrvojeM,Sekulla) . In this manifesto, a Moderator means every citizen who has access to eJahan's moderation panels and/or tools (Forum moderators, Technical moderators, Local moderators, Polices, Super moderators and Website guards). A Top moderator means any citizen who have one of top moderation posts (Super moderators and Website guards). All moderators are free to accept this manifesto, or resign from their moderation/ignore the registration invitation.

1. The main responsibility of moderators is to keep eJahan's players and income increasing. Profit is main reason for the administration team to create and support the game. More income means more motivation to update and upgrade server's hardware and software, longer stability of game and better support of it.

2. You - as a Moderator - chose to be a mod in first order, and a player at second. Things like My Country, My Alliance or My Enemies won't make sense in moderators' section. Don't forget that We, eJahan's administration team, have trusted you.

- Moderators are not allowed to use their power in any irrelevant case! Moderating tools has been made only for supervision on the game.

3. There is no second chance for multiple accounting cases, as it was before.

4. To ban a citizen for multiple account usage, you need: ISAM in Lens multi tracker (or 75% of multi suspect)

5. Trying to sell account will NOT result permanent ban for both sides, but because this action is illegal, you will have no responsibility about stealing accounts in these cases.

6. Giving violations which have 3 vio points or more will temporarily ban the citizen for a day, so if you gave the violation and didn't want to ban the citizen, you must unban him/her after setting the violation.

7. Forum moderators can request vio point for a citizen if the citizen has broken forum rules.

8. Political multiple accounts must get banned. Set ban for clones, and if you had enough proof that they belong to a candidate, arrest the candidate as well.

- Owner means a citizen account who collects the profit of multiple accounts. It may have less EP or less power than multiple accounts.
- finding multiple accounts during election, multiple accounts who joined parties to make that party in top 5 and all this kind of multies will consider as political multies will have a result of ban for clones and arrest for owner.
- Set ban for owner, only when a huge amount of multiple accounts usage is made. Refer to law 11 before doing this action!

9. Spamming in job offers (Setting many job offers to make chaos in job offers of a country, or getting a job and resigning for multiple times to remove the offer of a company) is considered as spamming, too.

10. Top moderators can not take ANY POLITICAL POST IN CABINET. They should choose to be a politician or a moderator. If they want to be politician, they can resign from moderation as long as they want to join a cabinet.

- Being congress member is not prohibited, but consider law 2 of manifesto.

11. before you take any measure, you should discuss about abuse of citizens on "Moderators section" and "Top Mods polls" section of eJahan forum and Channel #mods_private in eJahan IRC Server. But if you've got strong proofs, you can act without making a poll in forum. Anyway, beware that you'll be responsible for it.

12. Do not delete articles, unless there is something bad enough which force you to do so.

13. Citizens always can complain about mod abuse through sending a ticket to MASTER, if a moderator abuses his/her powers, MASTER can set 1 mod vio point against him/her. Mod vio points will not expire and if a mod receives 2 mod vio points, he/she will lose his/her access and receive 9 vio points.

14. Moderators are allowed to speak to citizen about eJahan moderating matters, ONLY through the official places (in tickets and Channel #mods_public in eJahan IRC Server).

15. To gather information about vio points for each abuse, refer to eJahan Laws, part 13.

16. The best moderator of month will receive 50 Tala pack for free. The administration team selects the best moderator via mod actions, specially mod comments on citizen profiles and citizen ratings in tickets.

17. If a citizen sent a ticket in a wrong department, or you couldn't process the ticket (like you couldn't decide about multiple accounts), you should move it to its correct department with the feature installed in Lens.

18. The administration team may change the laws. Please check the manifesto in eJahan Lens for possible updates.
